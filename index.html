How to Play

  • Words appear on screen. Type them to cast spells — each finished word hurls a bolt at your foe.
  • Red curse-words drift toward you. Type them before they reach you or you'll take damage. Your keystrokes automatically find the right word — just type what glows.
  • Wrong keys break your combo and cause backlash damage. Chain perfect words for combo multipliers and spell unlocks (1 Fireball · 2 Frost Nova · 3 Mend).
  • Bosses channel passages under a countdown — finish the passage in time for massive damage.
  • Esc pauses. Survive, level up between chapters, and restore the Great Lexicon.
